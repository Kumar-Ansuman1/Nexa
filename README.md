# Nexa

## AI Company Analyst

Nexa is an AI-powered company intelligence platform designed to help startups understand what is happening inside their business.

It analyzes structured and unstructured company data to discover patterns, generate insights, investigate causes, make predictions, and provide evidence-backed answers to business questions.

## Vision

Nexa aims to act as an AI analyst for a company by connecting its data and transforming it into actionable business intelligence.

## Core Capabilities

- Company data analysis
- Business insights
- Machine learning predictions
- Natural language processing
- Deep learning
- Semantic search
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- AI-powered business analysis
- Evidence-backed answers

## Development Approach

Nexa will be developed incrementally from the ground up.

The project will progress through:

1. Product and data foundation
2. Analytics
3. Classical Machine Learning
4. Natural Language Processing
5. Deep Learning
6. Embeddings and semantic search
7. RAG
8. AI Analyst
9. Backend and API
10. Frontend
11. Integrations
12. Evaluation and monitoring
13. Deployment
