Flowdesk Pricing Policy

Flowdesk offers three subscription plans.

Free:
Designed for individuals and very small teams.
Includes basic dashboard access and limited reporting.

Starter:
Designed for growing teams.
Includes advanced reporting, team management, and additional integrations.

Business:
Designed for larger organizations.
Includes advanced analytics, expanded integrations, priority support, and higher usage limits.

Customers may upgrade or downgrade their plans according to the applicable subscription terms.
