
# Nexa — AI Company Analyst

## Product Overview

Nexa is an AI-powered company intelligence platform that helps startups understand their business by analyzing structured and unstructured company data.

Nexa identifies important changes and patterns, investigates their possible causes, generates predictions, and provides evidence-backed answers to business questions.

## Problem

Companies generate large amounts of information across multiple sources, including customer data, product activity, transactions, sales records, support conversations, feedback, and documents.

Although this information exists, understanding what is happening inside the business often requires manually combining data from multiple sources.

Nexa aims to reduce this analysis burden by bringing relevant company information together and turning it into understandable, evidence-backed insights.

## Target Users

Initial target users:

* Startup founders
* Product managers
* Business/operations teams
* Growth teams
* Small data/analytics teams

Initial focus:

Small and medium-sized startups that may not have dedicated data analysts for every business function.

## Product Vision

Nexa aims to become an AI analyst for a company.

Instead of requiring users to manually inspect multiple data sources, Nexa should be able to:

1. Understand company data.
2. Identify meaningful changes and patterns.
3. Investigate possible causes.
4. Predict relevant future outcomes.
5. Answer business questions using company evidence.
6. Clearly distinguish facts, observations, predictions, hypotheses, and conclusions.

## MVP

The first version of Nexa will allow a startup to provide structured and unstructured company data and receive:

* A company overview
* Important business metrics
* Detected changes and anomalies
* Basic business insights
* Analysis of customer feedback and support data
* Answers to business questions using available company data
* Supporting evidence for important conclusions
